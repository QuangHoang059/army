QUAN LY VU KHI - HUONG DAN CAI DAT MAY CHU
==========================================

CAI DAT (chay tai cho - khong chep sang thu muc khac)
  1. Giai nen thu muc nay vao NOI DAT CO DINH tren may chu (vd D:\QuanLyVuKhi).
     Chuong trinh chay ngay tai thu muc do -> dung xoa / di chuyen thu muc sau khi cai.
     Khong can cai Node.js.
  2. Chuot phai CaiDat.bat > Run as administrator.
     - ALLOWED_IPS: lan cai dau tu lay dai mang cua card mang dau tien (vd 192.168.1.0/24).
       Cai lai / cap nhat: GIU NGUYEN ALLOWED_IPS dang co trong app\backend\.env.
       (ghi de: CaiDat.bat -AllowedIps "10.0.0.0/8, 192.168.10.0/24")
     - Chay backend :4000, giao dien :3000 (KHONG tu chay khi bat may, KHONG tu khoi dong lai)
     - Cai dich vu cloudflared (tunnel) bang tunnel-token.txt (bo qua: -NoTunnel)
  3. Mo http://localhost:3000  (may khac trong mang: http://<IP may chu>:3000)

CAP NHAT BAN MOI
  1. Chay GoCaiDat.bat (giu du lieu) de dung chuong trinh.
  2. Giai nen ban moi DE LEN cung thu muc (du lieu app\backend\data, backups, .env giu nguyen).
  3. Chay lai CaiDat.bat.

QUAN LY
  Log:           logs\backend.log, frontend.log, install.log
  Chay / CHAY LAI: ChayApp.bat   (dung ban dang chay roi mo lai backend + giao dien)
  Dung:           DungApp.bat    (hoac dong 2 cua so "QLVK Backend" / "QLVK Giao dien")
  Bat may xong:   phai tu chay ChayApp.bat (khong tu chay).
  Cau hinh:       app\backend\.env  (sua xong: chay lai ChayApp.bat)
  Bi chan IP:     trang bao loi hien "IP ..." -> them dung IP/dai do vao ALLOWED_IPS.
                  Luu y: dien thoai/mang co IPv6 thi IP hien ra la IPv6 (vd 2401:d800:...),
                  them IPv4 (113.185...) se KHONG co tac dung. Xem them logs\backend.log.
  Tunnel:         Get-Service Cloudflared  /  Restart-Service Cloudflared

GO CAI DAT
  GoCaiDat.bat              (go dich vu, xoa file chuong trinh, giu du lieu)
  GoCaiDat.bat -RemoveData  (xoa ca du lieu - KHONG khoi phuc duoc) -> sau do xoa ca thu muc
